# 🎯 START HERE - Warung Oma Website

## Welcome! 👋

This is the complete website and backend system for **Warung Oma**, a traditional food business in Manggarai Barat, NTT.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Open the Website
```
Double-click: index.html
```
The website will open in your browser!

### Step 2: Try Ordering
1. Scroll to "Produk" section
2. Click "Pesan Sekarang" on any product
3. Fill the order form
4. Click "Kirim Pesanan"

### Step 3: Login to Backend
1. Click the "🔐 Admin" button (top right or footer)
2. Login with:
   - Username: `admin`
   - Password: `admin123`
3. Explore the backend!
